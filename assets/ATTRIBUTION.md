- `archivo.ttf`: Archivo, The Archivo Project Authors; SIL Open Font License 1.1, reproduced in `archivo-OFL.txt`. Source: Google Fonts, `ofl/archivo/Archivo[wdth,wght].ttf`.
- `instrument-serif.ttf`, `instrument-serif-italic.ttf`: Instrument Serif, The Instrument Serif Project Authors; SIL Open Font License 1.1, reproduced in `instrumentserif-OFL.txt`. Source: Google Fonts, `ofl/instrumentserif`.
- `{app-id}.webp`, `*-hero.webp`: app icons and product images from the corresponding product pages at `https://getartcraft.com/apps/{app-id}`. Copyright and product identity remain with their upstream owners. Their use here identifies the apps being managed; the source-code license does not relicense them.
- `craftlauncher-logo.png`: original CraftLauncher identity generated with the built-in Imagegen tool; transparent PNG, used by the window, tray, sidebar and packaging. See `BRANDING.md` for its prompt. Same source-code license.
- `craftlauncher.png`: previous programmatically drawn launcher icon, retained as an earlier asset; same source-code license.

CraftLauncher is an independent launcher. It does not claim to be an official ArtCraft release or reuse the ArtCraft logo as its own identity.
